About

I'm Michael Ouellette, a Software Engineering Manager based in Pittsburgh, PA with 17+ years of experience specializing in frontend architecture, design systems, and web performance at enterprise scale. I currently lead multiple engineering teams at Dick's Sporting Goods, including the Homefield Design System — a multi-framework component library spanning React, Angular, React Native, and Web Components — and the Web Foundations team, where modernization efforts across large-scale NX monorepos have driven 32–68% bundle size reductions and significant core web vitals improvements.

I'm experienced in building and mentoring high-performing teams, defining technical direction, and delivering infrastructure that accelerates product development across the organization. If you'd like to connect, reach out through the contact page or any of the social links in the header.
